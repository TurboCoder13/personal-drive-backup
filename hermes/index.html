Hermes personal assistant

Hermes is a private, self-hosted AI assistant operated by TurboCoder13 for the operator's own use. It runs on the operator's systems and is reachable only through the operator's private Discord and Slack chats.

Google account access

With the account holder's consent through Google's consent screen, Hermes uses Google APIs to help manage the operator's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts — for example to read, search, draft, send or organise messages, events and files when the operator asks.

This assistant is for the operator's own use and is not available for public registration.
