Privacy policy

For the Hermes personal assistant. Last updated: 31 October 2026.

Purpose and data access

Hermes is a private assistant that uses Google APIs to work with the operator's own Google account. When the operator requests it, Hermes may access email messages and metadata, calendar events, Drive files, Docs and Sheets contents, and contacts as needed to complete that request.

Storage

Access credentials, conversation history, working files and selected notes may remain on the operator's systems and in the operator's backups. These remain until the operator removes them or they are replaced or rotated.

Use and sharing

To answer requests, relevant content may be sent to the AI model providers configured by the operator, which process it under their own terms. Responses, which may include parts of that content, may appear in the operator's connected private Discord or Slack chats. Google handles API access and authentication.

This public website does not receive Google OAuth tokens or Google account contents.

Revoking access

The account holder can revoke Hermes's access in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorised access; it does not delete records or backups already held on the operator's systems. To request deletion of those records, or for questions about access, contact the operator at the address below.

Website hosting and contact
